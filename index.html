Inside the canvas

Nine ways Canvy gets out of your way

Everything a whiteboard does, plus the parts a whiteboard can't: typed diagrams, photos that become diagrams, styles, presenting, share links and history.

Type a line, get a diagram

Write arrows the way you'd write them in a chat message. Canvy draws the boxes, spaces them out and keeps them editable, so you can drag things around afterwards.

Snap a photo, get an editable diagram

Photograph a diagram on paper, a whiteboard or a screen. Canvy finds the boxes, arrows and labels and reads the Thai and English text, then lets you fix any word it wasn't sure of before adding it. Printed text reads best; handwriting is read only partly. The photo never leaves your device.

One diagram, many looks

Switch the whole canvas between six looks: pencil, chalk, marker, blueprint, clean ink and neon. Your layout and labels stay exactly where they were.

Present without the clutter

Press Present and each frame becomes a slide, full screen with the toolbars hidden. Press N for speaker notes and L for a laser pointer. Esc takes you back to editing.

Export anywhere

Download a PNG for slides, an SVG for docs, a PDF with one page per frame, Mermaid code, or a .canvy file you can open on another device.

Good to know

Questions people ask

Where is my work stored?

In your own browser, on your device. Canvy has no server and no accounts, so nothing you draw is uploaded. Clearing your browser's site data deletes it, so save a .canvy file for anything important.

What happens to a photo I turn into a diagram?

It is read on your device and never sent anywhere. The first time, Canvy downloads the reading model, OpenThai2.0-OCR-Text, once (about 20 MB with its runtime) and keeps it for next time. Boxes and arrows are found even in hand-drawn sketches. Printed Thai and English text reads well; handwriting is read only partly, so Canvy lists every word for you to check before it adds the diagram.

What is inside a share link?

The diagram itself, compressed into the part of the link after #. Anyone with the link can open it, so share it the way you would share the file. Pictures you added are left out to keep links short.

Does it work in Thai?

Yes. The interface, templates and typed diagrams all work in Thai, with Thai word wrapping. Switch the language with the button at the top.

Can Claude or another AI draw in Canvy for me?

Yes. Connect Canvy as shown in the AI & MCP section above, then ask for a diagram. The AI writes it into a Canvy link that opens as an editable diagram. The diagram travels inside the link; nothing is stored on a server.

Does it cost anything, and does it work offline?

It's free. After your first visit, Canvy keeps working without an internet connection.
